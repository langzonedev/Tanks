Field manual

Move within your fuel allowance, set angle and power, then fire once. A short dotted trail shows the launch direction. Wind bends your shot. Craters remove cover and can drop tanks into lower ground.

Keyboard A / D to move, ← / → to aim, ↑ / ↓ for power, Space to fire. You can also drag on the battlefield to aim or use the controls.

Frontline Each side opens with a deployment turn. Move one unit or hold before combat unlocks. In combat, pick a unit, reposition, and issue one order. Hold the central zone through opposing turns to score four points or eliminate the opposing force. Air raids and reinforcement drops each have one charge per side.

Share turns After firing, send the next-turn link to your opponent. Opening that link reconstructs the match so they can take their shot and send a new link back.
